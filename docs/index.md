# Raising Cane's Nutrition Guide

Raising Cane's is known in the United States for a deliberately short menu built around chicken fingers, crinkle-cut chips, Texas toast, coleslaw and Cane's Sauce. Because the menu is so focused, it is easier than at many fast-food chains to understand what a meal contains and how its parts fit together, but the official nutrition information still matters for anyone watching particular nutrients, managing an allergy or simply comparing options before ordering.

This guide explains how to read that information sensibly. It looks at the main items in a typical Cane's meal, the role of sauces and sides, and the questions worth asking in the restaurant, such as portion sizes, customisation and allergen information. It also explains why figures can vary slightly between locations and why checking the latest official information before you visit is always the safest step.

For the full, up-to-date details, see the [Raising Cane's nutrition guide](https://theraisingcanesmenus.com/raising-canes-nutrition/).
